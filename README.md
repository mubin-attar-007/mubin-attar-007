### About

I'm an Applied AI Engineer with **3.5+ years** in software engineering and a production-AI focus since 2024. At **Sevina Technologies** I built three products that are in use: **Bench Discovery**, **CareLTC** and **SignFlow**. Their code is private; their case studies, on dummy data, are on [my portfolio](https://mubin-attar.vercel.app/work).

My public work follows one idea: **the guarantee lives outside the model.** A deterministic check, not the model, decides what may run, and every result I publish comes with its method, sample and date.
